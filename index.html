Chae Min Lee

Integrated M.S./Ph.D. student · Yonsei University College of Medicine

I am an integrated M.S./Ph.D. student in the Department of Medical Science at Yonsei University College of Medicine (Genomics and Data Sciences track), advised by Prof. Sungsoon Fang. My research is on multimodal machine learning for multi-scale biomedical data integration, linking molecular profiles with imaging and tissue-level phenotypes to improve disease stratification and biological understanding. Trained as a registered nurse, I aim to build translational AI that connects computational modeling to patient-relevant applications.
